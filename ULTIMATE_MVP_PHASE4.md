# Ultimate MVP — Phase 4

## Admin Unified Inbox

- Aggregates client, provider, direct-oversight, internal, and announcement channels
- Enriches every conversation with its academic order or marketplace service
- Shows client, provider, category, status, deadline, unread count, open decisions, files, and pending expenses
- Search across message text, people, IDs, titles, categories, channels, and tags
- Filters for unread, needs reply, client, provider, direct contact, decisions, expenses, escalated, and resolved
- Sorts by newest, oldest, priority, and deadline

## Conversation operations

- Full chronological thread
- Admin replies in the selected channel
- Mark-as-read when opened
- Priority and state controls
- Assigned administrator
- Follow-up date
- Escalation control
- Operational context for decisions, files, and expenses

## Root navigation

- Open the complete academic order or service workspace
- Click client, provider, or category to filter related conversations
- Return to files, evidence, expenses, receipts, quote changes, clarifications, and decisions

## Safety

- Requires authenticated admin session
- Client-provider direct messages remain controlled per job
- Admin may oversee an enabled direct channel
- Conversation changes are audit logged
