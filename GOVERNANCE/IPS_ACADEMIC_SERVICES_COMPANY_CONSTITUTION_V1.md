4. “New IPS” means **new implementations** of the *existing intended IPS Academic Services business*, not permission to invent a new industry, change the customers, or abandon requirements.
5. No agent may redefine the business without a specific new owner instruction. Whenever a design conflicts with this constitution, the design loses.
