# Ultimate MVP Phase 6 — Action Center

The Admin and Provider portals now include a unified Action Center below Messages.

Actions are generated automatically from files, decisions, expenses, assignments,
provider acceptances, unanswered job messages, rejected evidence and overdue work.

The Action Center includes role-scoped access, priority and overdue sorting, search,
filters, job deep links, file-release shortcuts, in-progress state, snoozing,
resolution and dismissal reasons, sidebar counts and audit records.
