# Ultimate MVP Phase 5 — File Governance and Evidence

- Client uploads stay visible to that client and administrators until released.
- Provider uploads stay visible to that provider and administrators until released.
- Uploaders can download their own non-archived files.
- Administrators choose the audience: client, provider, both, or admin-only.
- Rejections and archives require reasons.
- Files retain category, description, checksum, review state, audience, and timestamps.
- Upload and review actions appear in the job audit history.
